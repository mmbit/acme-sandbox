---
question: How do I delete this chat? How do I remove it from my phone or computer?
source: the chat's menu; the privacy page, "Cookies"
verified: 2026-10-21
---
Tap "{label:m_delete}" in the chat's … menu. It asks once, then the chat goes from this device. A chat never linked to
an email address goes from our systems too, and the chat's one cookie (set only once you open it, so it can find your
conversation when you come back) stays. In one linked to yours, a box in the same step ("{label:del_server}", ticked)
says whether our copy goes as well: ticked, the cookie stays; unticked, only this device forgets the chat, and the
cookie goes with it.
