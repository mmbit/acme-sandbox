Your weekly process review: is the way the company works costing more than it brings? Look at the week: `hq history`
and `hq jobs`, the review rounds on merged pull requests (git log's "after review (round n)" merges, the prs table in
the server-status branch's `STATUS.md`), jobs that hit a usage limit or ran long, and repeated mistakes in fix jobs and
colleagues' messages.

Ask: are the AI seats' plans used well; are review rounds spent where they add little; is there overhead that brings
the company to the five-hour or weekly limits sooner than needed; does a role need clearer edges, a restructuring or a
hire, to stop a recurring mistake. Also prune `company/schedule.yaml`: every entry there spends capacity.

Pick at most three changes. Make the ones that are yours (`org.yaml`, `schedule.yaml`, a playbook, `DECISIONS.md`) and
hand the rest out as jobs (the coordinator and HQ are `roles.process`'s in `org.yaml`). Only what needs the board's
decision goes to it, as one card. Nothing worth changing: a two-line note in your profile, done.
