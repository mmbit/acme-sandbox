Your weekly memory pass: reread what you keep about how to work here against how things work now, and rewrite or
remove what's no longer true, so stale pieces stop coming back into your calls and jobs. One short job, your own
judgment on your own memory. What belongs in memory and what doesn't is the Memory playbook's; in short:

- **Rules from the board stay** until the board changes them (a later call, card or decision that replaces one).
- **Beliefs about how the system behaves never go in memory** (how long reviews take, what holds work up, how fast a
  tool is, who answers when): they're looked up live when a question needs them, and a stored one is soon wrong. A line
  of that kind goes, or becomes the pointer where it's looked up (the command, the report's section, the note).
- **No size limit.** This pass does the pruning, not a cap.

**What you read and rewrite.**
- Your profile's working memory (`company/people/<you>.md`): the whole section, rewritten to what's true now. Keep what
  still holds and is worth knowing next time (a lesson, a method, a pointer); drop what's finished, what a later change
  made false, what's in a playbook or note already (point to it instead), and every belief of the second kind. Check a
  line you're unsure of against main and the live state (`hq report`, `hq search`), not your memory of it.
- The CEO also its page (`hq pad`): read it, then rewrite it whole on the version it read (`hq pad write - --version
  <id>`), the same way. Its working memory in the profile stays the one line pointing at the page.
- Your entries in `company/DECISIONS.md` that a later entry replaced: under each, one line `Replaced <date> by <the
  later entry: its date and a few words>`, so search shows it. The entry itself stays word for word: decisions and
  calls are history.

**What you leave.** Your track record (history, archived word for word by the docs sweep), other agents' profiles and
entries (tell their owner with `hq say` if one looks wrong), the charter, `AUTHORITY.md`, the board's own words.
Never move a customer's details or a key anywhere: if you find one, stop handling that line and tell whoever handles
security and the CEO at once (`hq say`, pointing to file and line only).

Nothing stale: change nothing, and end saying so. Otherwise one small pull request, content only, its summary listing
what you removed or rewrote and why.
