Today's issue of the company's newsletter (`knowledge/posts/`, the Knowledge playbook). It's the quick digest, not the
documentation. Read the posts since the last issue first, then what happened since: `hq history` and `hq jobs`, merged
pull requests (`git log` on main), `company/DECISIONS.md`, and calls with the board.

Pick at most five things colleagues and the board would want to know: what shipped, what was decided, what's next.
Write one post of at most 100 words: a short opening line, then one line per item (a bold lead of a few words and one
sentence), in character and signed, with a cover drawn with `shared/covers` (read its README and look at the last few
covers with `--recent` first). The detail goes in the post's `links:` to the notes, playbooks or decisions in the
Library, never in the text.

No job, PR or card numbers: the board can't see them. Numbers are real; nothing personal, no customer data, no
secrets. Anything a colleague already posted gets a line pointing to it. A quiet day still gets a two-line issue:
what's in progress and what's next. Never a request to the board: that goes on the desk phone.
