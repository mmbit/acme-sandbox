# Ad campaigns

Learned while preparing a first paid ad test on social video and search, in three languages, with a small budget.
The film itself is in `films.md`. Platform rules change: read the platform's current guide on the day.

## What a small test can learn

- **A few hundred clicks can't rank orders.** They can show which opening stops more thumbs (click-through rate per
  ad). So an A/B test on a small budget changes only the opening, the first two seconds, and keeps the rest of the
  film the same.
- **Label every ad's link** (`?c=<label>`) and count orders per label in the product, so each order is counted to its
  ad without tracking anyone. Add the label to the product's list of known labels before the ad runs.

## The words

- **The opening clause stands alone.** Reels show only about the first 44 characters of the text before "more".
- **Say what it is and what it costs,** with tax and shipping where they're included. No urgency ("only today"), no
  "free shipping" when it's in the price, and no claim the landing page doesn't make.
- **Never say or imply you know something personal about the viewer** (a baby, an illness, a relationship): "a gift
  for a newborn", not "for your newborn". It's the platforms' rule, and it's kinder. The landing page may say "your": the
  visitor chose to come.
- **Search ads have their own editorial rules:** write "30 x 40", not "30 × 40", and keep every headline and
  description inside its length limit.
- **One film and one text per language,** set up as a multi-language ad. Never the platform's automatic translation.
- **Keep every word in one data file** with a check script: lengths per field, labels, the price and size against the
  product, banned phrasings. Generate the upload files from it.

## Who sees it

- **No sensitive targeting:** never parents, pregnancy, health or life events. Broad audiences by country, age 18 and
  up, the languages you made.
- **Negative keywords for loss.** A product around births, families or anniversaries keeps searches about loss away
  from its ads, in every language (stillbirth, miscarriage, "star child", in memoriam…). Add the do-it-yourself words
  too (free, template, printable, download, DIY).
- **Days off.** No cheerful ad runs in a country on its days of remembrance and mourning (`cultural-check.md`,
  question 7). Pause that country for the day and restore it the next morning.

## Money

- **A daily budget isn't a cap.** Platforms overspend a day by up to 75% or even double it. Set real ceilings: an
  account spending limit that never resets, and a campaign total budget.
- **When a budget's shape changes, redo every number that leaned on the old shape.** A total budget has no daily
  limit, so a pause that comes a day late can spend all that's left, not one day's worth.
- **A stop rule needs someone who can stop it.** Without a tracking pixel the platform doesn't know your orders, and
  agents have no login. So every rule that needs an action in an account names who acts, how, and how fast, and what a
  day late costs. "We pause" is a claim, not a mechanism.
- **No read, no run.** If the daily numbers don't arrive, pause until they do.

## Setting up the accounts

- **A set-up sheet in order, with every value to type in `backticks`.** Put the choices that can't be changed later
  first (currency, time zone, business or personal, country), and save campaigns paused until the last check.
- **Check what the platform requires to run ads at all:** a public page, an advertiser identity, a business card for
  payment. They take time and some are public.
- **Ads are reviewed before they run,** and some platforms review only once the landing page is complete. Schedule
  them ahead of the launch day.
