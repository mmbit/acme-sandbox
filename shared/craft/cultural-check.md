# How it lands

A two-minute check that whoever made the work runs on it before it goes public: an ad, a product page, a picture, a
newsletter, a help page, an order email, a support template, or a product idea. It isn't a department and it isn't a
veto on playfulness. A company's own playbook may set its bar and its markets; where it does, follow that.

## The bar

Flag a **plausible, real risk**: something a sensible person in your markets would see as offensive or damaging
(blackface, a far-right flag, a slur in a name you print). The test: can you picture the angry headline or the hurt
customer's post actually happening? If you have to build a reading to get there, it isn't a finding.

Typos, date formats, translations and taste are ordinary product work, not this check. Someone given only this job
over-flags: most of what a dedicated audit finds doesn't survive this bar. Balance is part of the job, so brief a
helper with the bar and the seven questions, not only with "check it".

## The seven questions

Read the words aloud and look at the pictures as a stranger in your markets would. Most work passes all seven at a
glance.

1. **Blackface or caricature?** A dark face with big red lips, gold hoops, a servant role; caricatured features of
   any group. In the Netherlands and Belgium that includes any form of Zwarte Piet.
2. **A far-right or real-looking flag, symbol or number?** In Germany some are crimes: the swastika and look-alikes,
   SS runes, the Wolfsangel, the Black Sun, the imperial war flag, 88, 18, 1488. The far right also carries the Wirmer
   flag (a black Nordic cross edged in gold on red) and others. A "found your own country" joke stays obviously
   make-believe: no references to a real *Reich* or ID papers.
3. **A slur or rude word in a name you generate, or in customer text you print?** See `generators.md` and
   `shared/wordcheck/`.
4. **A joke at a group's expense** (religion, origin, skin colour, body, disability, age)? A concept that only works
   with a stereotype goes back.
5. **Whimsy where it hurts?** Births, weddings and deaths are where this bites. A cancelled order gets no celebration,
   and a reply to grief, fear or anger gets no joke.
6. **Aimed at children?** Talk to the adults who buy, even for a child's product. Nothing asks a child to want it or to
   nag, and nothing tells a child that Sinterklaas, the Christkind or Santa isn't real.
7. **The day it goes out:** a remembrance day or bad news? For example 4 May in the evening (the Netherlands),
   11 November (Belgium), 27 January, 9 November and the national day of mourning in November (Germany). Elections and
   wars are never a hook. Glance at the news on the day.

## The outcome

- **Fine:** ship it.
- **Fix:** change it and ship it. It's usually one word or one picture.
- **Real doubt:** ask one colleague.
- **A plausible risk that's a real judgement call** goes to the board as one short request with the options and a
  recommendation. Nothing implausible reaches the board.

Record one line where the work already is, for example in the pull request: `How it lands: fine` or
`How it lands: fixed, a generated name hit "nazi" → next variant`.
