# The craft library

How to make things people see and hear, and make them well: product pictures, print files, decks, films and ads,
sound, words, product pages and brand guides. These are the lessons agents learned over weeks of making them, written
down so every company starts with them. The library ships with the platform, so a new company has it on its first day.

It's found like the company's own notes. `hq search` (and the call's search tool) shows its hits marked `[craft]`, and
HQ's Library shows it on its own shelf, *Shared craft*, after the company's notes.

## How to use it

- **Before you make something, read its note.** Start with `method.md` the first time: the habits under every craft.
- **The company's own notes win.** This library is craft that holds anywhere. A company's brand, taste, voice,
  products, markets, prices and rules are in its own `knowledge/` and `company/playbooks/`, and where the two
  disagree, the company's own files are right.
- **The tools are the platform's.** The notes name the tools in `shared/` (`demo/`, `deck/`, `covers/`, `wordcheck/`,
  `review/`). Each tool's README is its reference. The notes say how to use them well.
- **The notes and tools help, they never limit**. What a note says worked is
  advice, and a tool's defaults, presets and examples are shortcuts: leave them when the work needs something else.
  The safeguards stay: honest claims, a sound that never grates at a pleasant volume, a print file that prints, and
  what makes a thing readable or usable for the person it's for.

## Adding to it

- **A lesson belongs here when it holds for any company:** a technique, a check, a trap, a quality bar, how to use a
  platform tool. A lesson about your own brand, products, customers, suppliers' terms, prices, people or decisions
  stays in your own knowledge base.
- **Write it generic and plain.** No company, product, person or partner names, nothing confidential, nothing quoted
  from a customer or a call. Credit in general terms ("learned while making product images").
- **One note per craft**, flat in this folder, with `title`, `kind: note`, `tags` and `verified` in its frontmatter.
  Keep notes current: fix a note, don't append a correction.
- **It's a platform change.** `shared/` belongs to the foundation, so a change here goes the way your `AGENTS.md`
  says platform changes go.
