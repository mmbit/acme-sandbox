# Showing work for a decision

Learned while showing products, brands, decks and films to the board and folding its feedback back in. How a
request to the board is written is in the company's own playbooks; this note is about the work you show with it.

## Show the thing

- **Finished and working beats described.** A preview to tap through, a film, a deck or a mockup says more than any
  paragraph. Show a mockup before building something big (`method.md`).
- **Made for the screen it's seen on.** Portrait first; every video with a poster; checked at phone size before it
  goes out (`decks.md`).
- **Work brought back for a look shows each change before and after,** the same frame twice at a phone's width
  (`shared/review/shot.mjs`), with the live link under them; the words go under More. The board looks and answers.
- **Options side by side,** with the same words and data, and your recommendation with its reason.
- **One clear decision per request.** The film, deck or preview carries the detail.

## Make sure it can be opened

- **What the board opens is served from main.** A film or picture for a request goes in after its merge, so send the
  request from a follow-up job queued `--after` the one that made it. When a change replaces a film under the same
  name, the old cut plays until the merge.
- **Use full addresses** for anything the request links to, and check the link works before sending.
- **A landscape film needs its reason on the request itself.**

## Review mode on previews

- **The board pins notes on a product's preview** (`shared/review/`): a typed or spoken note, the element, a
  screenshot and the last clicks. Only notes the board sends from HQ reach an agent: the CEO routes each to the
  colleague it's about.
- **Read the whole note:** `hq note <id>` gives the screenshot, the element's HTML and the trail. The screenshot can
  show a second bug the note doesn't mention.

## Pictures on the intercom and on calls

- **The board can attach or paste pictures** on HQ's intercom and during a call: a screenshot of HQ itself, a photo, an
  outside site it likes. The line or the transcript names each one, #<n>.
- **Look before you act:** `hq picture <n>` saves it as a file you can read. When you hand the work on, put the number
  in the brief: the colleague saves it the same way.

## Taking the feedback

- **Fix the thing, then look for it everywhere it shows:** the app, the ads, the deck, the help page, the posters.
- **When feedback reverses a decision, rewrite the instructions below it,** not only a status line above them. A file
  that says "cancelled" at the top and the old steps underneath sends the next job the wrong way.
- **Keep a round to its findings.** A change nobody asked for in a fix round is new, unreviewed work: queue it on its
  own.
